# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things  
**Genre:** Science Fiction / Mystery / Adventure  

**Why I Like It:**  
I really enjoy the strong friendship between the main characters and how they support each other through every challenge.  
The story is filled with mystery and excitement, especially when they explore the Upside Down.  
The combination of science fiction, friendship, and 80’s nostalgia makes the show unique and fun to watch.  
The character development is also amazing — you can really see how each one grows with every season.  
> “Friends don’t lie.” – Eleven

**Main Characters:**  
- Eleven  
- Mike Wheeler  
- Dustin Henderson  
- Lucas Sinclair  
- Will Byers
  
**Favorite Episode:**  
Season 1, Episode 8 – “The Upside Down”  
I love how it ends with both suspense and emotion, showing true friendship and bravery.
